# The Drowned Bell

*Chapter III · Bellwatch Harbor*

> [!QUEST] Active Objective
> Reach the tidal causeway before moonset and strike the **Seventh Bell** while the sluice gates stand dry.

### Objectives

- [x] Speak with Harbormaster Maren at the drydock
- [x] Recover the **tide charts** and `sluice_key` from the flooded office
- [ ] Equip ![Shield](icon://shield) **Tideguard Buckler** or carry a ![Potion](icon://potion) **Crimson Tonic**
- [ ] Ring the Seventh Bell at low tide
- [ ] Return to Maren with the salvaged clapper

> [!LORE] Archivist's Note
> The harbor charter lists only *six* storm bells cast in 1794. Whoever hung the seventh under the breakwater left no foundry mark—only salt-eaten tally notches inside the watchtower doorframe.
